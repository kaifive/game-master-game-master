# Game ideas

A parking lot for games Khai might want later (2026-11-17). Nothing here is
planned until it has a spec. Each idea says what it's like, and which solo
game trains it where that fits.

## Team showdowns (TV shows it, captain's phone controls)

| Idea | How it plays | Trained by |
| ---- | ------------ | ---------- |
| **Spot the Swap** | Two near-identical pictures (club photos) sit side by side on the TV; teams tap where the difference is on the phone's copy. Fastest right tap wins. | Color Matcher, Block Fit |
| **Echo** | The TV plays a growing sequence of colored lights (and stances: horse, bow, cat); teams repeat it on the phone. One mistake and you're out; last team standing wins. Like Simon. | Rapid Recall |
| **Zoom Out** | A tiny crop of a photo slowly zooms out on the TV (a medal, a weapon, Coach's shoe); teams buzz in with a guess from four choices. Earlier right answers score more. | Trivia |
| **Green Light** | The TV shows red… red… then green; first to tap after green wins, tapping on red knocks you out for the round. A pure reaction duel. | T-Timer |
| **Tug of Present** | Two teams at a time mash their phones; a present slides toward the faster team on the TV. Best of three pulls. | Tap Masher |
| **Stack Off** | All teams play Stack Attack at once on their phones while the TV shows every tower growing side by side. Tallest tower wins. | Stack Attack |
| **Move Match** | The TV shows a wushu move as a silhouette for a second; teams pick its name. Gets faster each round. | Wushu Trivia |
| **Higher or Lower** | Club stats on the TV ("Years Coach has taught", "Medals at Pan Ams"); teams guess if the next is higher or lower. Keep the streak alive. | Trivia |
| **Price Is Close** | Guess a number about the club (fans in the gym, push-ups Coach can do); closest without going over wins. | Guessing Jar |
| **Two Truths** | Three statements about a coach or athlete; teams pick the lie. | Trivia |
| **Draw-ffle** | One player draws a wushu word on the captain's phone, the drawing shows live on the TV, and every other team guesses it. | Quick Draw |

## Solo games

| Idea | How it plays |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Odd One Out** | A grid of near-identical icons; tap the one that's different, faster each round. |
| **Sequence Lock** | Remember a short string of stance icons, then tap them back in order. |
| **Balance** | Hold the phone flat to keep a present on a beam (device tilt), for as long as you can. |
| **Word Ladder** | Turn one wushu word into another a letter at a time, each step a real word. |
| **Sort It** | Swipe items left or right into two groups (weapon or stance, northern or southern) against the clock. |
| **Rhythm Strikes** | Tap in time with drumbeats; on-beat taps score. |
| **Photo Pairs** | A memory card game with club photos: flip two at a time to find pairs. |
| **Count the Kicks** | A short clip of a form plays once; how many kicks were in it? |

## More team showdowns (2026-11-17)

Like Land Mass, the best ones make teams play against each other, not just
side by side.

Now in spec 24 (2026-11-17): Present Shuffle (from Shell Game), Auction (from
Blind Bid), Snowball to 21, Stocking Stuffer and Snow Sum.

### Outsmart the other teams

| Idea | How it plays | Trained by |
| ---- | ------------ | ---------- |
| **White Elephant** | Wrapped presents with hidden point values sit on the TV. On its turn a team opens a new one or steals an opened one from another team (a present can be stolen twice, then it's locked). Values are revealed at the end. The holiday game. | (none: pure strategy) |
| **Blind Bid** | Each round a prize card (worth 20 to 100) goes up; every team secretly bids from a shared budget of 100 for the whole game. Highest bid wins it; tied top bids both lose. | Guessing Jar |
| **Snowball to 21** | A shared snowball count starts at 0; teams take turns adding 1, 2 or 3. Whoever makes it exactly 21 wins the round. A real strategy you can figure out, so it gets better each round. | (none: logic) |
| **Tower Topple** | One shared tower on the TV; teams take turns dropping a sliding present on it, Stack Attack style. Each row narrows; the team whose present misses loses points, and everyone who placed before them scores. | Stack Attack |
| **Trap Path** | Memory Path, but before it starts each team secretly hides one trap square for the others. Step on a trap and you're out; your own trap is safe for you. | Pathfinder |

### Fast hands

| Idea | How it plays | Trained by |
| ---- | ------------ | ---------- |
| **Grid Grab** | Numbers 1 to 30 light up on a TV grid one at a time; the first team to tap that square on their phone claims it. Claim squares in a row of three for a bonus. | Rapid Recall |
| **Stance Call** | The TV calls a stance and a direction ("Horse! Left!"); tap the matching picture. Wrong or last is out; it speeds up until one team is left. Like Simon Says. | Wushu Trivia |
| **Musical Mats** | Music plays and the TV shows mats; when it stops, tap a mat. One fewer mat than teams each round, and two teams on the same mat both bounce. Musical chairs. | (none: luck and nerve) |
| **Snowball Shot** | A target slides across the TV; tap to throw when it's lined up. Hits score, and a bullseye freezes another team's next throw. | T-Timer |

### Push your luck

| Idea | How it plays | Trained by |
| ---- | ------------ | ---------- |
| **Stocking Stuffer** | Each turn, open a stocking for points (5 to 30) or bank what you have. Pull a lump of coal and you lose everything you didn't bank this round. | (none: nerve) |
| **Ice Bridge** | Two tiles per step, one cracked; teams pick left or right to cross, and a wrong pick drops them. Teams who go later learn from the falls before them. | Memory Path |

### Watch closely

| Idea | How it plays | Trained by |
| ---- | ------------ | ---------- |
| **Snow Sum** | Snowflakes fall on the TV with numbers and signs (+3, −2, ×2); keep a running total in your head and type the answer. | Rapid Recall |
| **Mirror Me** | Half a pattern lights up on a grid; tap the mirror image on your side. | Pathfinder |
| **Unblur** | A club photo starts blurred and slowly sharpens; buzz in and pick who or what it is. The sooner, the more points. | Trivia |
| **Shell Game** | Coach's medal goes under one of three presents, which shuffle faster each round; pick the right present. | Rapid Recall |

## Even more team showdowns (2026-11-17)

Now in spec 24 (2026-11-17): Share or Snatch, Last Second (a visible
countdown: stop as late as you dare) and Color Count (from Count the Reds).

### Mind games

| Idea | How it plays |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Lowest Lonely Number** | Every team secretly picks a number from 1 to 20; the lowest number that nobody else picked wins. Pick 1 and hope nobody else dared. |
| **Share or Snatch** | Teams pair up over a pot of points; each secretly picks Share or Snatch. Both share: split it. One snatches: they take it all. Both snatch: nobody gets it. |
| **Odd One Wins** | A silly question with three answers ("Best weapon for a snowball fight?"); teams in the smallest group score. Guess what the room won't pick. |
| **Hot Cocoa** | A hot cocoa mug sits with one team; on your turn pass it to any team. A hidden timer goes off, and whoever's holding it loses points. |

### Board games on the big screen

| Idea | How it plays |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Dots and Boxes** | A dot grid on the TV; teams take turns drawing one line. Close a box to claim it and go again. Most boxes wins. |
| **Ornament Drop** | A tall grid like Connect Four, but with every team: drop an ornament in a column on your turn; four in a row wins the round. |
| **Snowball Strike** | Each team secretly hides three presents on its own small grid; teams take turns lobbing a snowball at another team's grid. Hit them all to knock that team out. Like Battleship. |
| **Hide the Elf** | One team hides an elf on a grid; the others take turns guessing a square and hear hotter or colder. The longer it stays hidden, the more the hiders score. |

### Eyes and ears

| Idea | How it plays |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **What Moved?** | A holiday scene on the TV blinks, and one ornament has moved; tap where. |
| **Count the Reds** | A flash of mixed ornaments; how many were red? (Or gold, or stars.) |
| **Carol Clip** | Two seconds of a carol, sped up or backwards; pick the song from four. |

### Off the phone

| Idea | How it plays |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Freeze** | Music plays and the up player dances holding the captain's phone; when it stops, freeze. The phone's motion sensor catches anyone still moving. |
| **Strike a Pose** | The TV calls a stance or move; the up player does it for real. The host taps the best ones in Controls. |
| **Forehead** | The up player holds the phone to their forehead with a wushu or holiday word on it; the team shouts clues, and tilting down means got it. Most in 60 s wins. |
