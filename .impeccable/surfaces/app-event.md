## Scope

Player client and TV display for the Wushu Holiday Party (event `holiday-2026`), Experience mode. Style pack: Judges' Table, variant: winter. Admin screens are out of scope (standard admin theme).

## Audience and job

Club families at a dim holiday party, kids to grandparents, on their own phones, playing games in any order; the room watches the TV. Seconds to fun; scores visible and fair.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The party scored like a taolu competition on a snowy night. Every result is a judge's score card held up; the TV is the results board. It refuses the party-app default of bright pills and bubbly type.

OWN-WORLD: Cranberry judges' band with a gold rule and holly; white score cards with black Oswald numerals on candy-striped handles; frosted card backs for unscored; snow dots on a frost-white (day) or winter-night ground. Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading.

STORY: Players see what's up for grabs, play in any order, and watch each result flip up as a score card; the room watches team results climb on the board.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hub: judges' band; name and team left, total in large red numerals right; progress pips; then up for grabs, scored and coming up, each row ending in its own score card; "Enter a code" last. TV: band across the top, results table left, toast chain and join panel right.

FORM: Judges' Table, the user's choice after three rounds (my top candidate in round 3), refined with a winter variant. seed:5d69cbb2968fb07f

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

The flip: score cards flip up on their handles when a score lands; snow drifts on the TV when big moments land, with a gong on the display.
